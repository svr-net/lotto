Ensemble picks

These tickets combine all strategies. Each strategy votes with the numbers it would play. The equal-weight ensemble counts every vote the same. The performance-weighted ensemble trusts strategies more when they matched more numbers over the last 52 rounds. The stacked ensemble learns its weights: a logistic regression, trained on the rounds already played and refitted every 26, learns how much each strategy's vote says about whether a number is drawn. Every line has the same 1 in 45,057,474 chance of a jackpot.
